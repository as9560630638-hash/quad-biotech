Encapsulation Science

What are
Softgel Capsules?

Softgels are ideal for liquid or semisolid fills, making them highly suitable for the delivery of oil-based formulations. Softgel capsules are translucent capsules with liquid or semi-liquid solutions hermetically sealed within gelatin shells.

Fully customisable shape and size to fit your formulation needs
Enhanced bioavailability — ingredients absorb faster & more completely
Accommodates liquids, semi-liquids, suspensions, and emulsions
Easy to swallow — no water required for most formulations
Airtight hermetic seal that is tamper-evident and oxidation-resistant

Key Advantages

Why Choose
Soft Gelatin Capsules

Enhanced Bioavailability
Softgels disintegrate rapidly and significantly improve the absorption of active ingredients compared to tablets and hard capsules.
Tamper-Evident Sealing
Hermetic seals protect active ingredients from oxidation, moisture, and microbial contamination — problems are visible through leakage or discoloration.
Versatile & Customisable
Wide variety of shell colors, shapes, and sizes available for product differentiation. Fill can be semi-solid, liquid, gel, paste, or emulsion.
Precision & Consistency
Provides greater stability, purity, and uniformity of nutrients encapsulated — ensuring every dose delivers exactly what's promised.
Easy to Swallow
Smooth exterior and reduced odor make softgels the preferred choice for patients of all ages, improving compliance and adherence.
Clinically Preferred
Health professionals administer drugs in softgel form because they offer wide advantages over conventional medicines — faster onset, better tolerability.
